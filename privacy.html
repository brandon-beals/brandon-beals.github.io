Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Family Calendar is a private tool used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and writes events on the owner's Google calendars and lists the calendars the owner's account can see. It requests no other Google data.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is processed only on the owner's own computer and sent only to Google Calendar. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
